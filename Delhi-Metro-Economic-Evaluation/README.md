# Economic Evaluation of Delhi Metro Phase 4A

**Course:** CE 776 — Transportation Project Evaluation and Decision Making
**Programme:** M.Tech in Transportation Systems Engineering, IIT Bombay · Spring 2025
**Team Project** (Group 30)

## Objective

To assess the economic viability of Delhi Metro Phase 4A — a 61.7 km, 3-corridor, ₹24,949 crore metro expansion — through a formal cost-benefit analysis and to produce a policy-ready appraisal recommendation.

## Brief Description

A full Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA) of the Phase 4A expansion was carried out, computing the Economic Internal Rate of Return (EIRR), Net Present Value (NPV), and Benefit-Cost Ratio (BCR) under both discounted and undiscounted assumptions, incorporating direct and indirect socioeconomic benefits.

## Methodology

- Estimated project costs and quantified direct and indirect benefits: travel-time savings, vehicle operating cost (VOC) savings, and emissions/safety gains.
- Computed EIRR, NPV, and BCR under discounted (12%) and undiscounted scenarios.
- Found an EIRR of 19.81% (undiscounted) and 6.97% (12%-discounted), with a BCR of 5.7 and 1.99 respectively.
- Validated economic viability through multi-scenario sensitivity analysis and synthesized findings into a policy-ready project-appraisal recommendation.

## Tools Used

Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA), EIRR/NPV/BCR computation, sensitivity analysis.
