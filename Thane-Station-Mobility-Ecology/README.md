# Mobility Ecology of Thane Railway Station

**Course:** PS 651: Mobility Policy and Infrastructure
**Programme:** M.Tech in Transportation Systems Engineering, IIT Bombay · Spring 2025

## Objective

To study the operational and governance "mobility ecology" of Thane Railway Station, one of Mumbai's busiest interchange stations (6-7 lakh daily passengers), through a systems-level lens, and to produce actionable recommendations for passenger safety and station management.

## Brief Description

Primary field research (a station-manager interview plus on-site observation) was used to analyze the station through a Hardware-Software-Practice systems framework, mapping how infrastructure, digital systems, and operational practice interact across passenger management, emergency response, and digital integration.

## Methodology

- Conducted a station-manager interview and structured on-site observation of passenger flow and infrastructure.
- Applied a Hardware-Software-Practice systems framework to map three interdependent operational systems: passenger management, emergency response, and digital integration.
- Identified three critical pedestrian conflict zones through targeted flow analysis.
- Developed recommendations (a crowd-management plan, emergency-preparedness upgrades, and AI-based crowd-density monitoring) grounded in the multi-agency governance context of Indian Railways, municipal authorities, and transit-oriented redevelopment.

## Tools Used

Qualitative field research, stakeholder interviews, systems-framework analysis.
