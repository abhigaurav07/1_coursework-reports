# Geometric Design and Analysis of the Simaria-Matihani Greenfield Bypass Road, Begusarai District, Bihar

**Course:** CE 773: Geometric Design and Analysis of High-Speed Roadways
**Programme:** M.Tech in Transportation Systems Engineering, IIT Bombay · Spring 2025

## Objective

To design and evaluate candidate greenfield bypass alignments for the Simaria-Matihani corridor in Begusarai District, Bihar, in order to divert through-traffic off NH-31 and improve regional connectivity, in compliance with IRC design standards.

## Brief Description

Two candidate bypass alignments were designed and modeled end-to-end in AutoCAD Civil 3D using DEM-derived terrain data: a 4-lane, 100 km/h alignment (9.98 km) and a 2-lane, 80 km/h alignment (15.66 km). The design directly supports a regional policy target of reducing NH-31 traffic volume by 38-42% and improving industrial/agricultural connectivity for a district of ~1.9 million people.

## Methodology

- Derived terrain and corridor data from a Digital Elevation Model (DEM) for route corridor generation.
- Engineered horizontal and vertical alignments, spiral transition curves, and superelevation profiles for each candidate route, per IRC:73-1980 and IRC:SP:84 standards.
- Quantified cut-fill earthwork volumes for both alignments (~129,600 m³ and ~293,200 m³ respectively).
- Compared alignment corridors against villages, existing infrastructure, and route overview constraints to select a preferred design.

## Tools Used

AutoCAD Civil 3D, DEM-based terrain modeling, IRC:73-1980 / IRC:SP:84 design standards.
